The First Asian Cartographic Conference(AsiaCarto2024)는 International Cartographic Conference의 지역행사로, 아시아 최초의 ICC Regional Conference입니다. 연구실에서는 4편의 논문이 accept되어 발표하였습니다.

- Juyeon Cho, Youngok Kang, "Anomaly Detection and Spatiotemporal Analysis of Pedestrian Trajectories Using VAE"
- Jaeyeon Choi, Seungmin Noh, Somang Kim, Youngok Kang, "Evaluating Urban Walkability: Analyzing Physical Walking Environment Using Street View Images and Deep Learning"
- Jiyoon Lee, Youngok Kang, "Road Congestion and Safety Algorithm for Alleyway Utilizing Trajectories Based on CCTV"
- Somang Kim, Jaeyeon Choi, Youngok Kang, "Measurement and Visualization of Perceived Fear of Crime by Street Level using Street View Images and Deep Learning Technology"
